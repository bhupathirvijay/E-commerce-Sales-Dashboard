E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | YTD Sales Analysis

I built this interactive Tableau dashboard to give leadership a quick view of year-to-date e-commerce performance. KPI cards for sales, profit, and order quantity show current figures and the change against the prior year. Year-to-date sales reached $14.11M, up 5.04%, while profit held roughly flat at $1.30M (-0.46%).

The dashboard also breaks performance down by market and product category. Latin America and Europe together account for about 89% of sales. Camping & Hiking (+12.55%) and Water Sports (+21.10%) grew year over year, but most other categories declined, which points to where attention is needed. A monthly ranking chart shows how each market’s customer acquisition shifted over the year, and Market and Customer Segment filters let viewers explore the data themselves.

Tools: Tableau
